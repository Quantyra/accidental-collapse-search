## Adaptive implementation-error revision, November 15, 2026

The auxiliary result is `TV <= min{1,sqrt(K) sum_j delta_j + P tau}`, with K=min{P+1,ceil(1/epsilon)} (P+1 at zero). It compares the same capped controller and exact oracle under ordinary-gate operator-norm errors and, separately, a collapse-rate error with the entire policy frozen. Source: S270 commit `05ac15549bae16a28c2d08c96feff698a2666de5`, proof SHA-256 `4d74e7a032b020a162779c2f5fe5fa54babed90be5cbec4f2d34d419502dee30`. The named transfer questions are whether prior gate-set independence or conditional-copy continuity already gives this precise persistent-feedback/collapse-sensitive modulus, and which effective-description assumptions make precision imply efficient compilation. Its three intermediate-state comparisons were inspected, not the entire cryptographic theorem. This supersedes the earlier abstract-only screening limit for that specific claim.

[Dawson--Nielsen quant-ph/0505030v2](https://arxiv.org/html/quant-ph/0505030v2), Sections 2-3, supplies the inherited fixed-dimensional synthesis mechanism under universal finite inverse-closed gate-set assumptions. Description evaluation, finite-arithmetic error, printing the synthesized word, all repetitions, memory and transcript bits remain charged. P011 v0.3.0 already supplied the local power comparisons and history/frontier machinery. The new proof changes the inserted operation to a perturbed gate and uses normalized history mass. Accordingly this is an attributed auxiliary consequence, not a new adversary framework, first continuity principle or new synthesis algorithm. It closes the paper's explicit general implementation-transfer caveat, with no claim of optimal parameter dependence, physical fault tolerance or ordinary BPP/BQP progress.
